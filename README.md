# Hello, I'm Nohemi Flores 👋

I am an **Analytical Researcher & Data Specialist** working at the unique intersection of database query architecture, investigative research, and visual storytelling. Blending a background in journalism with a business analytics framework from the C.T. Bauer College of Business, I excel at consolidating fragmented data streams, isolating operational risks, and translating complex mathematical math into intuitive visual interfaces and clear strategic narratives.
